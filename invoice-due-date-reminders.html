How to track invoice due dates

An invoice's issue date is not always its payment deadline. Read the payment terms and use the stated due date. If the document gives a relative period, confirm the correct calendar date before setting a reminder.

Capture or import the invoice into Pocket Triage. Select or customize a template for the information you need, such as supplier, invoice number, amount and due date. Check the extracted values before saving.

Set a reminder for a date that gives you time to review and arrange payment. Use a category or consistent tag to find related invoices later. If you use a calendar as your main schedule, open the calendar action and review the event before saving it.

After paying, keep your payment confirmation where you can find it. Do not treat a reminder notification or stored invoice as proof of payment. Pocket Triage organizes information; it does not execute bank transfers or reconcile your accounts.
