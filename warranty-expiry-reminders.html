Important date guide

How to track a warranty expiry date and set a reminder

Keep the receipt and warranty terms easy to find, then set a reminder early enough to review what you may need to do.

Find the date in the actual terms

A purchase date is not always the warranty start date, and the coverage period can depend on registration, product type or local terms. Read the warranty document and seller or manufacturer conditions. If there is no clear expiry date, contact the provider or choose a date you want to remember; do not treat an AI suggestion as proof of coverage.

Save the proof and warranty details together

Capture the receipt and warranty card or terms. Use a suitable document template, review any extracted product, merchant, serial or date details, and correct mistakes against the original image. Add the records to a consistent category and use a tag such as the product type or household location to find related documents.

Choose a reminder schedule

Pick a date that leaves time to inspect the product, locate serial numbers and contact support. A reminder a month before expiry may suit a product that needs preparation; a week or a day may be enough for a simple follow-up. Confirm the event date and reminder time before saving. Android notification permission, battery settings and device schedules can affect delivery.

If you also add the date to a calendar, review and save the event in the calendar app. The calendar entry and the app reminder are separate records.

Keep a useful record

Store the original image with the structured details, then search by the product, merchant or tag when you need it. Pocket Triage can help organize and remind you, but it does not extend a warranty or decide whether a claim qualifies. Verify requirements with the manufacturer or seller.

  1. Scan the purchase proof and warranty terms.
  2. Check the expiry date against the actual document.
  3. Save the record with a descriptive title and tag.
  4. Add a reminder at a useful lead time and confirm notifications are allowed.
  5. Search for the record once to ensure it is easy to retrieve.
