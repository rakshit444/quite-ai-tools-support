How to set a warranty expiry reminder

Keep the proof of purchase and warranty terms together when possible. The purchase date and warranty expiry date may differ, and coverage can depend on registration or other conditions. Read the actual terms before choosing a reminder date.

Capture or import the paperwork in Pocket Triage and select a suitable template. Review extracted information. In the reminders widget, confirm the suggested date or choose the date explicitly if nothing reliable was extracted.

Choose a reminder early enough to check the product and gather information. Review the scheduled date and time, enable reminders and save the record. Allow notifications in Android. If you add an event to a calendar, review and save it in that calendar app too.

Before making a claim, find the saved record and compare its details with the original paperwork. A reminder helps you remember to act; it does not extend coverage or guarantee that a claim will qualify.
