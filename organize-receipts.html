Receipt organization guide

How to scan and organize receipts on Android

A receipt is useful when you can find it and confirm what it says. A repeatable scan-and-review habit makes both easier.

Capture the details you may need later

Receipts often matter for returns, warranties, reimbursement or expense records. Capture the whole receipt, including the merchant, date, total and any tax details. Use a clear, well-lit image; if the paper is faded, keep the original and check the result carefully.

With Pocket Triage, choose the receipt template, take a photo or import an image, and let AI fill editable fields such as merchant name, amount, currency, tax and date. AI can misread text, so check each important value against the image before saving.

Choose categories for broad groups

Categories work well for stable groupings such as household, travel, meals or office expenses. Pick labels you expect to use again. Avoid creating a new category for each individual purchase; that makes reports harder to compare.

Use tags for projects and occasions

Tags can connect records across categories. For example, add the same trip tag to a hotel, restaurant and transport receipt. A project tag can bring together work purchases from different merchants. Keep spelling consistent so search and filters return the full group.

Find and export the records you need

Search saved records with a merchant name or useful keyword. For a summary, choose a date range and filter by category or tag before exporting. Check that the selected records are appropriate for the purpose; an exported report does not replace original documents or employer requirements.

Keep purchase dates actionable

For a product with a return period or warranty, save the proof of purchase and warranty terms together when possible. Verify the actual deadline from the seller or manufacturer; terms differ. Add a reminder for a date you confirm, rather than assuming every policy can be inferred automatically.

A five-minute setup

  1. Scan a recent receipt you expect to need again.
  2. Review the merchant, total, currency and date against the image.
  3. Add one broad category and any relevant project or trip tag.
  4. Search for the saved record to confirm your wording is easy to find.
  5. Export the records you need for a report, and back up your library before replacing your device.
