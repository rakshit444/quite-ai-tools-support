How to organize receipts on Android

Start with the receipt you are most likely to need again: a purchase with a warranty, a reimbursable expense or an invoice-related payment. Photograph the whole receipt in good light with dates and amounts readable. A sharp image makes reviewing extracted values easier.

In Pocket Triage, capture or import the image and choose the receipt template. Review the merchant, amount, currency, tax and date before saving. Preserve the original receipt image so the structured record can be checked against it.

Choose a category that reflects how you will look for the record later. Add a small number of useful tags, such as a trip or project name. Consistent names are easier to search than creating a new tag for every document.

Find records using search, then use report filters to review a particular date range, category or tag. Before replacing or resetting your phone, export a full backup and keep it in a safe location. A saved record is not a substitute for any original documents your employer or relevant authority requires.
