Privacy, in plain language.

Sprout Spell is a children’s spelling game operated by QuiteAiTools. An adult manages sign-in; children do not need their own email account.

Effective 7 November 2026. Covers Sprout Spell for Android and these support pages.

Account and security information

Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, account identifier, sign-in provider, verification status and authentication credentials to create and secure the account. Google sign-in can also supply your basic profile, including name and profile image. We do not request your Gmail messages, contacts or Drive files. Google may process IP addresses and technical request information for authentication, security and abuse prevention.

Learning information on your device

Selected age guide and level, words introduced or completed, answers, attempts, hint counts, active practice time, story rewards, detailed history and settings are saved in app-private storage on your device. An optional PIN is stored as a salted derived value. Detailed answers and reports are not uploaded to a learning server or synchronized between devices. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local information.

Account access and purchases

Firebase, Firestore and Google Cloud services store the account identifier, random mission identifiers, level and input mode, completion receipts, free-use allowance and access status to authorize activities and handle retries. The selected age and answer text are not part of these access requests. Service logs can contain technical request data, IP addresses and error codes.

Purchases are not yet enabled for this beta. When enabled, Google Play processes the payment; we do not receive payment-card details. Purchase tokens, product identifiers, ownership, renewal or expiry status and verification timestamps are processed to verify and restore access and prevent reuse. Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Optional usage analytics — on by default

Firebase Analytics starts enabled on a device with no saved choice. Turn it off under Settings → Usage & privacy. A saved off choice survives app updates. This does not reduce your free allowance, rewards or access.

We record limited events such as app opens, mission starts and completions, hint use, answer retries, access errors and changes to analytics settings. Only bounded counts accompany these custom events. We do not put names, emails, account identifiers, PINs, selected ages, words or answer text into custom analytics events and do not set an Analytics user ID.

The SDK also collects automatic app/session and device information, an app-instance identifier and information such as approximate geography derived from IP address. Automatic purchase-related events may be collected when purchases are supported. These analytics are pseudonymous, not anonymous. Advertising identifiers, advertising storage and ad-personalization signals are disabled. The app has no ads.

Switching analytics off disables future collection and resets local analytics data. It does not erase already uploaded reports or stop necessary account, access and security processing. Because Analytics identifiers are not linked to account IDs, account deletion does not automatically identify and remove past analytics events.

Children and family choices

We do not ask for a child’s name, email, date of birth, precise location, photos or voice recordings. Voice guidance uses installed offline text-to-speech voices and does not record microphone audio. There is no chat, public child profile or targeted advertising. Optional PIN protection is available in Settings. Email verification confirms control of an email address; it is not an independent age or parental-identity check.

An adult can contact us to review, correct or request deletion of account information, ask about data associated with use of the app, or request that collection stop. If a child supplied personal information without appropriate adult involvement, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

Storage, retention and deletion

Account information is kept while the account exists. To delete it, open Settings → Your account → Delete account → Delete permanently. Sign out and sign in again if a recent sign-in is required. After the service confirms deletion, the sign-in identity and that account’s local profile on this device are removed. Local copies on other devices must be removed there.

After deletion the access service retains minimal account-linked free-use totals and purchase-ownership deletion records to prevent reuse and fraud. Raw reusable purchase tokens are removed from deleted purchase records. These minimal records currently have no automatic expiration. Contact us to request review or deletion of retained information; we will explain any applicable retention requirement. Google Play may retain its own transaction records.

Analytics event retention follows the dedicated property’s retention settings; aggregated reporting can remain longer. Provider security logs and backups follow Google’s retention controls and may not disappear immediately after deletion. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and related obligations. Contact us about a specific deletion or retention request.

You can also request account deletion without the app by following these instructions. Removing your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play to stop renewal.

Service providers and disclosures

Google Firebase, Google Cloud and Google Play process the information described above and may process it outside your country. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s privacy policy. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Information may be disclosed when legally required or to address fraud, service abuse or security incidents.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts these support pages and may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure them. We add no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and updates

Contact QuiteAiTools at support@quiteaitools.com for access, correction, deletion and privacy questions. We update this policy’s effective date when it changes and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required for material changes.
