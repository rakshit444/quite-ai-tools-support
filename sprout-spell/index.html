A spelling adventure from QuiteAiTools

Little words.
Big adventures.

Listen, build words and help Pip reconnect six story islands.

Sprout Spell is an Android spelling game with 1,000 US-English practice words across 24 levels. Flexible starting-age guides from 5 to 10 appear in Settings, while play focuses on words and skills.

Practice with a purpose

  • Build with letter tiles or try alphabet recall.
  • Use spelling hints and offline voice guidance.
  • Correct answers advance automatically.
  • Complete deliveries to build a story path and earn interactive gifts.
  • See accuracy, hints and active practice time in Progress and history.

About the closed test

The app is being prepared for invitation-only Google Play testing. An adult-managed, verified account is required. Ordinary accounts receive three completed missions free, with no automatic charge. Approved tester accounts may have full test access. Purchases are not yet available in this beta; planned offers are US$2 per month or a US$24 one-time lifetime unlock. Do not make a payment outside Google Play.

Learning progress is stored on the device and is not restored after uninstalling or switching devices. Internet is needed for account and access checks. Ages and levels are practice guides, not a certified school curriculum. There are no ads, chats or public child profiles.

Settings that stay simple

Choose the starting age guide, learning level and input mode. PIN protection is optional. Usage analytics starts on by default and can be switched off in Settings → Usage & privacy.

Send feedback or request test access. Please avoid sending children’s personal details.
