Your account. Your choice.

Delete your Sprout Spell account in the app, or ask us to help without reinstalling it.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Sprout Spell and go to Settings → Your account.
  2. Unlock your optional Settings PIN if you enabled one.
  3. Choose Delete account, then Delete permanently.
  4. If asked, sign out and sign in again, then retry within five minutes.

Request deletion by email

Send an email from the address used for Sprout Spell to support@quiteaitools.com with the subject “Sprout Spell account deletion.” Ask us to delete your account and related data. We may ask you to verify control of that email to protect your account. Never send a password, payment-card number, PIN or child’s personal details.

What is deleted and retained

Deletion removes the Sprout Spell Firebase sign-in account and, when performed in-app, its local profile on the current device. Email requests cannot remotely erase offline files; clear app storage or uninstall on your devices to remove those copies. Other QuiteAiTools apps are unaffected.

Minimal free-use totals and purchase-ownership deletion records remain for fraud prevention and to prevent reuse; these currently have no automatic expiry. Reusable purchase tokens are removed from deleted purchase records. Google’s logs, backups and transaction records follow its retention controls. Previously uploaded analytics is not automatically linked to the deleted account. You may separately request review or deletion of retained data; we will explain any required retention. Read the privacy policy for details.

Account deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription. If you have one, cancel it in Google Play to stop future renewal. This beta currently has no enabled purchases.

Feedback and other requests

Contact support@quiteaitools.com for access, correction, learning feedback or other support. Include the app version and a description of the problem, and avoid unnecessary personal information.
