For parents and guardians

Privacy, in plain language.

Sprout Math is a children’s maths game provided by Quite Ai Tools. A grown-up manages the account and learning settings; children do not need a separate email account.

Effective 11 October 2026 · Covers the current Android preview (version 0.6.8) and these support pages.

What we use and why

  • Parent account: Google Firebase Authentication processes your email, account identifier, sign-in provider, verification status and authentication credentials to create and secure your account. Google sign-in can also provide your basic profile, such as name and profile picture. Sprout Math does not ask for access to your Gmail messages, contacts or Drive files.
  • Learning and settings: activity results, accuracy, time spent, hint counts, progress, rewards, drawings, approved paths and settings are saved on your device. In this preview, detailed progress and drawings are not uploaded to a Sprout learning server or synced between devices. Parent PIN protection is stored locally as a salted derived value.
  • Support: if you email us, we process your email address and the information you send to answer your request. Please avoid sending children’s names, photos or other unnecessary identifying information.
  • Service security: Google processes technical information, including IP addresses, to operate authentication and prevent abuse.

Children’s information and parent control

Children use a parent-managed account. The app does not ask children for their name, email, date of birth, location, photos or voice recordings. It has no chat, public profiles or advertising. Voice guidance uses installed offline text-to-speech voices; the app does not record microphone audio.

A parent PIN protects learning choices and settings. Parents can contact us to review or delete account information, ask about information associated with their child’s use, or request that further collection stop. See the deletion instructions below. An account email verification confirms control of that email; it is not a separate age or parental-identity check.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics are off by default. A grown-up may enable or disable them in Parent settings → Usage & privacy. Free play does not require enabling analytics.

When enabled, Firebase Analytics may receive mission starts and completions, help taps, retries, drawing-board opens, access-blocked events, service-error counts and saved-plan events. Custom details are limited to numeric path identifiers, counts and accuracy percentages. The SDK also processes app/device information and an app-instance identifier. These measurements help us understand how the app is used and improve activities; they are not a formal assessment of a child’s ability.

We do not attach account email, Firebase user ID, child names, PINs, drawings or typed answers to custom analytics events. Advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled. Turning analytics off stops new collection and resets the local analytics identifier; it does not erase information already received by Google.

Who processes information

We use Google Firebase Authentication for sign-in and optional Google Analytics for Firebase for usage measurement. Google may process data on servers outside your country. Read Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect the service or to address fraud and security issues.

Storage and deletion

Local learning data remains on the device until you remove the Sprout profile, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Keep anything you need before deleting; this preview does not provide cloud progress recovery.

Firebase retains account information while your account exists. To delete the Firebase sign-in account, email support@quiteaitools.com from the account email. We verify ownership and process the request. The current in-app “Remove Sprout profile” action removes the local profile and signs you out; it does not delete the Firebase identity.

Firebase states that authentication IP logs are retained for a few weeks. Provider logs, backups and any previously collected analytics are subject to the provider’s retention and deletion controls and may not disappear immediately when an account is removed. Contact us about a specific deletion request; we will explain any information that must be retained and why. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle requests and related obligations.

This website and payments

These pages use GitHub Pages hosting. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the website. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Paid checkout is not enabled in the current app preview. We do not collect payment-card details. We will update this policy to describe payment processing before paid plans become available.

Questions, requests and updates

Contact Quite Ai Tools at support@quiteaitools.com for privacy questions, access/correction requests or deletion. If you believe a child supplied personal information without appropriate parental involvement, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

We will update the effective date when this policy changes and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required before materially changing how information is used.
